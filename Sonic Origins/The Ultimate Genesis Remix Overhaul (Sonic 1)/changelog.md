## Version 1.4

Music is now being UPGRADED!! The intent is to remake all tracks using DefleMask like I do for all Genesis Remixes now, rather than the old way with GarageBand. This means that the music is more accurate to the original hardware!

However I will not be doing it all in one release - it will roll out over the course of several releases, of which this is the first.

The following tracks have been upgraded:

- Marble - it is now the full version of "Back in Time"
- Labyrinth
- Star Light - it is now the full version of "Living in the City"
- Act Clear
- Game Over - replaced with a version of the Sonic Advance series' Game Over theme
- Continue
- Title - it is now "Knight of the Wind" from Sonic and the Black Knight
